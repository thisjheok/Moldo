# Sample 2

## Questions

1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

2. Tell me about the banks in your country. Where are they located? What do they look like? When do they open and close?

3. What do you do when you go to the bank?

4. Have you ever had a problem at the bank? Where did you go? When was it? What exactly happened, and how did you solve the problem?

5. You indicated that you travel domestically. Tell me about a place you like to visit. Describe the place, and also tell me why you like to visit that place.

6. Tell me about a trip you took in your childhood. Where did you go? Who did you go with? What did you do there?

7. Tell me about a memorable or unforgettable thing that happened when you traveled in your own country. What happened? What made the experience memorable to you?

8. I would like to know about your house. How many rooms are there? Also, tell me about your favorite room.

9. Tell me about some problems you have experienced in your house. Has something ever been broken? Or has someone you lived with not cooperated well with you?

10. Tell me about one of the problems you've mentioned above. What was the problem, and how did you deal with it?

11. You are going to visit your friend who lives abroad. Call your friend and ask three or four questions about the country, including its geography, to help you plan your trip.

12. You were supposed to travel abroad and visit your friend who lives there, but something happened to you, so you cannot go. Call your friend, explain the situation, and suggest two or three alternatives.

13. That's the end of the situation. Have you ever traveled overseas? How is the geography of the place you visited different from your country's geography?

14. What is your favorite kind of movie? Why do you like to watch those types of movies?

15. I also enjoy watching movies. Ask me three or four questions about the kinds of movies I like to watch.
