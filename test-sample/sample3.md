# Sample 3

## Questions

1. Let's start the interview now. Tell me something about yourself.

2. What is the weather like in your country? Also, tell me about the seasons.

3. How is the weather today where you are? What is happening now?

4. How has the weather changed in your country over time? Was the weather different when you were a child?

5. I would like to know about your house. How many rooms are there? Also, tell me about your favorite room.

6. Tell me about some problems you have experienced in your house. For example, it could be noise from your neighbor, something that was broken, or someone you lived with who didn't cooperate with you.

7. Tell me about one of the problems you've mentioned above. What was the problem? How did you deal with it?

8. Tell me about typical restaurants in your country. What are they like? What kind of food do they serve?

9. When was the last time you ate out? Where did you go? Who did you go there with? What did you eat at the restaurant?

10. Tell me about a special dish you had at a restaurant. How was the food? Was it memorable?

11. I'd like to give you a situation and ask you to act it out. You want to watch a movie with your friend. Call a theater and ask three or four questions about the movie tickets.

12. I'm sorry, but there is a problem I want you to resolve. You just found out you've been sold the wrong tickets. Ask someone at the movie theater for a solution, giving him or her two or three alternatives.

13. Have you ever been in a situation where you had a problem with buying tickets or making a reservation? What was the situation, and how did you solve the problem?

14. You indicated in the survey that you take vacations domestically. How is traveling different now compared to the past? Do you think traveling is easier or more difficult now than before?

15. Tell me about some issues concerning traveling. How do people handle those issues or problems?
